How to use

  1. Type the service name and the date its free trial ends.
  2. Choose how many days before to be reminded (3 is the default).
  3. Press Remind me. Reminder cards appear here as the date gets close.
  4. Press Add to calendar to get the same alerts as phone alarms.

FAQ

Does it message me by itself?

No. It has no server, so cards appear when you open this page. For alerts while the page is closed, use Add to calendar. It adds a separate calendar event at 9 am on each reminder day, plus one at 7 pm the evening before. The Google Calendar button adds a single reminder to Google Calendar. Charges are assumed to start at midnight of the trial end date.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Clearing site data deletes it.

What is Export / Import?

Export downloads your list as a small file. Import loads it back, for example on a new phone or after clearing your browser.
