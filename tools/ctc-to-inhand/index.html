How it works

🧾

Your annual CTC is split into fixed pay and variable pay.

🏦

Employer PF and gratuity are removed, because they are part of CTC but not paid monthly.

💵

Your own PF, professional tax and income tax are deducted. What is left is your in-hand pay.

FAQ

Why is my real salary slip different?

Companies structure pay differently (basic %, allowances, PF rules, insurance, TDS timing). Change the inputs above to match your offer letter for a closer estimate.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser, and nothing is saved or uploaded.

Is variable pay included in the monthly figure?

No. It is shown separately as an annual amount after the extra tax it causes.
