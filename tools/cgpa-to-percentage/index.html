FAQ

Which formula should I use?

The one your university or the organisation asking for it specifies. Many places accept CGPA × 9.5 when there is no official rule, but that is not universal.

Does it work for a 4-point scale?

No. This tool is for 10-point CGPA systems.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.
