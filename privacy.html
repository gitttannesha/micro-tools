Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

Micro Tools does not run a server-side backend for its tools. Everything you type into a calculator (CTC, CGPA, trial dates, etc.) is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to us or stored on our servers. The Free Trial Reminder tool saves the reminders you add in your browser's local storage, on your own device; this data never leaves your device and we never see it.

Cookies and advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as aggregate page-view counts) to understand which tools are useful. Any such analytics does not identify you personally and is not linked to the inputs you type into the tools.

Children's privacy

Micro Tools is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
